# Evergreen candidates

An Evergreen candidate preserves the reader-approved proposition exactly as supplied. Record the proposition, reason, origin kind, and origin paths; do not paraphrase, infer, or synthesize a Take. Candidates are pending work until a later specialist discussion closes them with the resulting note path and iteration.

Use the CLI's `evergreen add`, `find`, and `update` help for exact flags and output formats. Candidate writes are recoverable through the work lifecycle. A candidate's origin (backlog row, hunch, or surfaced tension), verbatim proposition, reason, and origin paths belong in the Evergreen work `synthesis-map.json`; do not duplicate that lifecycle state as an ad hoc direct write from the specialist.
