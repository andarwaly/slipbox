# Bounded lookup body

A Reference note is a compact answer to “what is X?” It contains enough
information for a future reader to identify the same reusable referent and
distinguish it from its nearest likely confusion.

## Required shape

- A clean H1 containing the subject name.
- One concise definition stating what the subject is.
- The essential characteristics or components needed to recognize it.
- Optional disambiguation only when a common confusion would make lookup unreliable.

The subject kind may be a concept, framework, tool, event, or creative work.
Adapt wording to the subject; do not create subtype templates or require fixed
headings for any one kind. Functionally dependent framework steps stay inside
the framework's one natural-unit note.

## What does not belong in the body

Do not add mandatory `Sources`, mechanism, application, implications, or `Open
Questions` sections. Do not organize the body source by source, reproduce a
source dossier, or turn the note into an evergreen synthesis. Provenance is
recorded in configured frontmatter only.

## Boundedness test

Stop when the definition and essential characteristics let a reader answer
“what is X?” and retrieve the same X consistently. Add distinctions only when
omitting them would cause a likely lookup confusion. Do not expand into a
history, tutorial, review, exhaustive taxonomy, or collection of examples.
