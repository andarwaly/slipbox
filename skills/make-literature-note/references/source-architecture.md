# Source architecture — whole-source reasoning, never written to disk

Six optional lenses for reading a source's shape before or alongside claim discovery,
feeding Core Idea formation specifically. A Core Idea synthesized purely from a bag of
already-discovered claims risks missing the source's own throughline — often carried by
structure and motivation as much as by individual claims. This file's scope is the whole
source; `qew-theory.md` covers a different grain, the per-claim reasoning that decides
whether one passage is claim-worthy and checks one Conclusion once it's drafted.

None of this becomes note content. Like Q/E/W, it is internal reasoning only — a way of
looking at the source, not a checklist to fill in or a template the note has to satisfy.

**Every lens below is optional.** A source lacking a piece — no stated resolution, no
named tension, no explicit motivation — is a valid finding about that source, not a gap
to force-fill. Never invent a tension, a motivation, or a resolution the source doesn't
actually have just to complete a group.

## Situation & Starting Point

What is the source's motivation, context, or purpose — what is it reacting to, building
on, or responding to? Where does it begin intellectually, before it starts arguing
anything? A source's justification for its central idea is as load-bearing as the idea
itself (already the Surface pass's own instruction on setup/motivation claims); this
lens is what surfaces that justification before claim discovery starts, rather than
after.

## Problem/Tension

What tension does the source identify, and which shape does it take? Notice the specific
form rather than defaulting to a generic "problem":

- contradiction
- trade-off
- gap
- mismatch
- bottleneck-shift
- unintended consequence
- paradox
- tension between principles
- ambiguity
- model failure

Naming the actual form sharpens what the source is doing far more than "the source
identifies a problem" ever does.

## Argument Movement

How does the source get from its starting point to its point? Conceptual-move chains;
premise → consequence → argument dependency; causal structure; reframing; contrasts.

**Argument dependency** — before treating a step in a chained argument as its own
peer-level claim, ask: would the rest of the argument still stand if this step were
deleted? If deleting it collapses what comes after, that step is a premise the later
claim depends on, not an independent claim sitting beside it. The same test already
governs whether a candidate justifies its own admission elsewhere in this family,
applied here inside one source rather than across sources.

**Repetition vs. reinforcement** — an idea the source restates five times in five
places is usually one claim stated five ways, not five claims. This is the same
shared-Warrant merge test the Surface pass already runs, spotted here through a
different signal: restatement rather than shared inferential structure.

## Support & Boundaries

What is doing supporting work, and what is fencing the argument in? Definitions and
distinctions the source draws; what job each example is doing — illustration,
counterexample, or evidence, since the same example does different work depending on
which; qualifications and caveats; objections and the source's responses to them;
competing positions the source argues against or alongside.

## Fidelity Signals

Kept as its own group, not folded into Support & Boundaries — this is where a claim's
epistemic weight actually gets checked, not one more thing to notice among many.

- **Epistemic stance** — does the source say "X is," "X suggests," or "X may"? A
  Conclusion drawn from a hedged passage needs to carry that same hedge, not firm it up
  into more certainty than the source itself claimed.
- **What the source doesn't establish** — the boundary of what the source is actually
  willing to claim. This is also where a genuine Open Question candidate comes from: a
  gap the source itself leaves unclear, ambiguous, or unanswered is exactly the kind of
  thing worth noticing here, surfaced to the user rather than silently smoothed over.

## Resolution

What does the source do with the tension it raised? Does it resolve it, resolve it
partially, or leave it open? **"Left unresolved" is a valid, complete finding** — not
every source closes its own loop, and a note shouldn't manufacture a resolution the
source itself never reaches.
